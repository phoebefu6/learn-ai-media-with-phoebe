Six 45-minute sessions, single track, and **one sixty-second video that gets genuinely better
across all of them**.

## What is real and what is modelled

`assets/media-live.js` holds a sixty-second audio envelope. When you press detect, it genuinely
scans that envelope for runs below 0.085 amplitude lasting at least 0.4 seconds and reports what
it finds: **four gaps totalling 12.4 seconds**, taking the cut from 60s to 47.6s. Those numbers
are computed at the moment you click, not stored. Change the threshold and the answer changes.

The retention curve is a **teaching model**, labelled as one on the widget itself. It is
directionally honest and it is not a measurement of anyone's audience.

That distinction is enforced throughout. Physics scores come from published benchmarks. Caption
error rates come from a 221-hour peer-reviewed study. The iteration cost per usable shot is
deliberately absent, because no independent audit of it has been published.

## The currency problem this course exists to teach

**OpenAI has exited video.** The Sora app shut on 26 April 2026 and the Sora 2 API sunsets on
**24 September 2026**, with an empty replacement column on OpenAI's own deprecations page. Every
course teaching "Sora, Veo and Runway" as the three pillars is already wrong. There is also no
Veo 4, Google having launched Gemini Omni instead, and every Google video model is still Preview
after Veo 2 and Veo 3 were shut down with fifteen days' notice.

Session 1 opens with this rather than hiding it.
